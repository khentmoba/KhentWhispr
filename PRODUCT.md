# SpeakFlow

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

A Windows desktop dictation application: hold a hotkey, speak, and insert text into the active application.

## Users

People dictating into their everyday Windows applications. The user confirmed a quiet, focused everyday workspace.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve local and cloud recognition engines, AI providers, dictation, translation, assistants, subtitles, history, correction rules, and per-app settings. Tauri, React, TypeScript and Rust are the existing stack. The interface is English-only, including built-in prompts and errors; multilingual recognition and user-authored content remain supported. Do not remove provider integrations or third-party license notices.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the SpeakFlow name. Replace the existing UI with a quiet, focused desktop experience. Build directly in code, as confirmed by the user.
